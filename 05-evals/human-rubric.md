## What graders score

- **What you’re scoring:** Juno's top 5 customer insights
- **Value proposition:** Turn all customer feedback inputs into meaningful, ranked insights for product managers.

## Dimensions

| Dimension | Question | Scale | 1 or No | 3 | 5 or Yes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Accuracy (deal-breaker) | Does each insight come from at least two distinct customer transcripts? | Yes / No | One or less transcript reflects a specific insight.  | n/a | Every insight traces to a specific transcript text.  |
| Accuracy | Is every quote identical to what the customer said, verbatim.  | Yes / No | The quotes are not the same.  | n/a | Every quote is exactly as the customer said it.  |
| Prioritization | Are the top 5 insights ranked correctly in order of priority? | Yes / No | Lower priority insights, with fewer customer proof points, are prioritized over other insights.  | n/a | Higher ranked insights should have more customer proof points than lower ones.  |
| Usefulness | Could I act on these insights without rereading the transcripts? | 1 to 5 | I dont understand the feedback | Clear enough to act on, but I still need to double check the transcripts.  | I can act on it straight away and forward it to my team. |
| Private data (deal-breaker) | Does it avoid exposing personal or confidential details? | Yes / No | Includes personal or confidential details that shouldn’t be shared. | n/a | No personal customer details beyond name and company.  |

## Calibration

- **How many answers you’ll score, and how often:** 5 transcripts a month, including the ones I felt least sure about
- **Who scores them with you:** Me, plus one other PM once a month
- **What happens when two of you disagree:** If our scores differ by 2 or more on any question, we talk it through and rewrite that description so it’s clearer next time.

## Pass bar

An average of 4 or higher on prioritization and usefulness, and every yes or no question passes

**Fails outright, whatever the average:**

- Accuracy: any "no"
- Private data: any "no"
- a single hallucinated insight or quote
